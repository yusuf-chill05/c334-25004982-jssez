03 / Plan with care

Incentives are real.
So are the conditions.

Treat incentives as something to qualify for and manage. The playbook recommends aligning them to the operating model, not the other way round.

INCENTIVES / PLAYBOOK, PP. 3, 15

Know what may apply.

  • Corporate tax: The overview lists a 5% rate for 10 or 15 years for qualifying companies with capital investment (excluding land) of at least RM500 million, and RM1 billion in the manufacturing sector.
  • Knowledge workers: A 5% personal income tax rate is listed for qualifying knowledge workers.
  • Other support: Sector-specific incentives, grants and facilitation may be available from both sides.

These are not automatic entitlements. Confirm current eligibility and conditions with the relevant authorities.

TAX & TRADE / PLAYBOOK, PP. 16-17

Keep people and goods in view.

  • Track where employees work and what they do. The JS-SEZ does not remove domestic tax or immigration rules.
  • Keep supplier declarations, bills of materials, processing records and HS codes consistent across both sides of the border.
  • Check rules of origin before claiming preferential tariff treatment. Incorrect declarations can mean extra duties, penalties or delays.

Plan for your actual activities. The playbook notes that incentives and compliance requirements differ between Singapore and Malaysia.

04 / Bring the right people

A pass for the
work being done.

Work out the purpose and duration of each visit before choosing a route. Pass rules and requirements can change.

Playbook, Appendix, pp. 28-29
EMPLOYMENT PASS

For an approved role

MIDA assesses expatriate positions in manufacturing, selected service sectors and approved regional establishments or offices. Posts may be Key (permanent) or Term (temporary).

Approval depends on the applicable criteria and the availability of Malaysian expertise.
PROFESSIONAL VISIT PASS

For specific, short-term work

For temporary professional services or training on behalf of an overseas company, such as consultancy or machine installation. The playbook describes a stay of up to 12 months and says it is not extendable.

It does not provide long-term employment rights.
INVESTOR PASS

For business travellers

For eligible foreign business travellers visiting to do business in Malaysia. The playbook describes stays of up to 12 months, in periods of up to six months, with a possible extension subject to requirements.

Applications are made online through Xpats Gateway.
